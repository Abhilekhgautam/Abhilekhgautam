# Abhilekh Gautam
 
I'm into compilers and I mostly write C++. I like working on optimizations and intermediate representations, and lately I've been trying to understand how compilers for hardware work.
  
## What I've been building

**[Stream Dialect](https://github.com/abhilekhgautam/stream-dialect)**: dialect for streaming hardware accelerators.
  
**[pytorch-to-calyx](https://github.com/Abhilekhgautam/pytorch-to-calyx)**: takes a PyTorch model and turns it into Calyx, an IR for hardware accelerators. It's a long way from `import torch` to something you could put on an FPGA, and this is my attempt at covering that distance.
 
**[wats](https://github.com/Abhilekhgautam/wats)** and **[compiler-optimization](https://github.com/Abhilekhgautam/compiler-optimization)**: a front end generator and a pile of optimization passes, both for Bril, a small IR that's great for learning how compilers actually work.
 
## Lately
 
Reading and poking around in [CIRCT](https://github.com/Abhilekhgautam/circt) and [Calyx](https://github.com/Abhilekhgautam/calyx), trying to understand how hardware compilers are put together.
